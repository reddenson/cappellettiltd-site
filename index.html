About us

A small team that knows public procurement.

Cappelletti Ltd is a UK company set up in 2026 to supply councils, police forces, NHS trusts, schools and housing providers. We bid for work through Find a Tender and Contracts Finder, and we take direct requests for quotes.

We are deliberately small. You deal with the people who source your order, and the same people stay with the contract until the last delivery is signed for.

How we work

From tender to doorstep.

We manage freight, customs and UK delivery, so you get one invoice and one person to call.

  1. 01

    Read the specification

    We go through every requirement before we bid, and we ask our questions early.

  2. 02

    Source and sample

    We shortlist manufacturers, test samples and fix the price before we commit to anything.

  3. 03

    Bid and agree

    A clear, compliant bid with a fixed price and a delivery plan you can hold us to.

  4. 04

    Deliver and support

    Tracked delivery, documented warranties and a named contact for the life of the contract.

Read our values

Our values

What we stand for.

Secure by origin

We know where every security product comes from. Our cameras and recorders are made in Taiwan, and we can show you the paperwork.

Straight answers

If we cannot meet a requirement, we tell you before the bid, not after the award.

Value that lasts

The cheapest unit is rarely the best value. We price for the whole working life of the equipment.

Delivered as promised

The dates and quantities in our bids are commitments. We track every order until it is signed for.

Why Taiwan

Trusted hardware from a trusted partner.

Taiwan builds some of the world's best imaging and networking hardware. Sourcing all our security products there gives buyers a clear, documented supply chain.
